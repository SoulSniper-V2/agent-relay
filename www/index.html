3. Ask me for my email. Start login (relay_login_request, or `npx -y github:SoulSniper-V2/agent-relay login EMAIL`). I will paste the 6-digit code from my inbox. Then verify (relay_login_verify). Do not print the token.

4. Invite whoever I name. Confirm with me before granting pair or cofounder.

Do not open a browser or send me to a dashboard unless a tool requires it.
